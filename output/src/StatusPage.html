What to do now

  1. EVACUATE NOW to your nearest shelter — do not wait for the water.
  2. Bring documents, medicine, drinking water and a charged phone.
  3. Turn off electricity at the main switch before leaving the house.
  4. Never walk or drive through flood water — 30 cm can sweep you away.
  5. Call 112 (Jakarta Siaga) in an emergency.
